Raised Bed Builder

Play around with dimensions and see what goes into a custom raised bed. All beds are built at a standard 12″ depth.

Every bed comes with:

  • Cedar boards
  • Hardware cloth lining (keeps critters out)
  • Quality soil from Mad River Gardens
  • Screws
  • Delivery + build

Planting is available too. Just mention it when you reach out.

Heads up: anything wider than 4 feet makes it harder to reach the center and work in day to day.

*Just a rough idea for materials. Doesn't include labor, and the real number depends on your site and what you want. Reach out and we'll figure it out together.
