About me

Hey, I'm Taya.

A barefoot Florida kid who never stopped playing in the dirt. I studied permaculture in Costa Rica, ran a specialty farm in North Carolina — building a mushroom grow and microgreens operation from scratch — and learned gentle beekeeping right here in Humboldt.

I came for the redwoods and stayed for the people. In spring 2026, Garden Faery bloomed: hands-on, organic garden care across Arcata, McKinleyville & Eureka. I treat your garden like it's my own.
