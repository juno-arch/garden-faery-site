Cyclical living

Usha Anandi's framing: these principles hold across the whole cycle, not just one phase.

Every meal is medicine

Food isn't neutral fuel. Each meal is information your body reads and uses to build the next month's cycle. Eat like you're already pregnant (even if you aren't) — sleep well, eat regularly, add sweetness: soft fabrics, beauty, rest, pleasure.

The 48-minute rule

Eat your meals at the same time every day — give or take 48 minutes. Your metabolism entrains to the rhythm. Chaotic meal timing destabilizes insulin, cortisol, and eventually reproductive hormones.

Solar alignment

Your digestive fire follows the sun.

  • Breakfast: light (sun's still rising, fire's small)
  • Lunch: largest meal of the day (sun at its peak)
  • Dinner: light, early (fire winding down, body needs to repair)

A heavy late dinner sits on top of a cold fire and disrupts sleep + hormone repair overnight.

Fasting is not made for women's bodies

Usha: "Fasting diets might be trendy, but they weren't made with women in mind." What she teaches happens when women fast:

  • Day 1: cortisol spikes, body stores belly fat, blood sugar crashes → shaky + irritable
  • Day 2: insulin + leptin destabilize, agni (digestive fire) weakens, bloating + gas
  • Week 1: muscle breaks down, metabolism slows, period goes irregular or stops
  • Month 1: hair thins, skin dries, immunity weakens, paradoxical weight gain from slowed metabolism

Eat regularly. Add healthy fats. Your reproductive system needs fuel to stay online.

Digestion is the campfire

A strong fire burns cleanly and efficiently. Signs yours is unbalanced: irregular hunger, feeling undernourished even after eating, fatigue after meals, early fullness. Fix digestion first — then fertility, mood, skin, everything else follows. Add more healthy fats with your meals to absorb fat-soluble nutrients (A, D, E, K) and support hormone production.

Survival > reproduction

Your body always picks survival first. Chronic stress diverts energy away from sex hormones (estrogen, progesterone) toward stress hormones (cortisol, adrenaline). That's when cycles go irregular, periods get painful, or ovulation stops entirely. Create a cocoon — drop draining relationships, cut stressful media, protect sleep. Your ovaries are listening to your nervous system.

31 days

It takes roughly 31 days for nourishment from food, herbs, and lifestyle to reach your reproductive tissues. Traditional preparation for conception is six months to a year. This is slow medicine. Trust the rhythm.

Yoni steam

The word yoni is Sanskrit for womb / vulva / sacred portal. The practice is ancient — traceable across Mayan, Korean, Russian, African, and indigenous curanderisma traditions. You sit over a pot of hot herbal water, wrapped to the waist in a blanket. Steam carries the herbs' volatiles to your vulva and cervix.

What it's for

  • Softening + supporting postpartum recovery (under midwife guidance)
  • Gentle womb care between cycles
  • Quieting stuck, stagnant, or heavy cycles (traditional use)
  • Ritual, slow, attentive time with the body that brings life

When to steam

  • 3-5 days before your expected period (late luteal) — reset
  • OR 3-5 days after your period ends
  • 1-2x per month, not more

When NOT to steam

  • During your period (don't heat active bleeding)
  • During pregnancy — ever
  • With an IUD (steam can damage it)
  • With active infection or open wounds
  • If you run hot / have hot flashes / excess heat conditions

How to do it at home

  1. Bring 2-3 quarts of water to near-boil with a handful of dried herbs
  2. Steep 10 min covered, then check temp with your wrist — comfortable warm, never hot
  3. Set pot on the floor or a low stool
  4. Remove underwear, sit over the pot on a chair or commode
  5. Wrap a blanket around your waist + legs to trap the steam
  6. Stay 15-20 min. When it stops feeling warm, you're done.
  7. Bundle up, rest, drink water for an hour after

Herbs (pick 3-5)

Rose — heart opening
Mugwort — gentle flow (mindful)
Calendula — lymph, gentle
Lavender — calm, spirit
Yarrow — womb tone
Raspberry leaf — tonic
Chamomile — soft nervine

Your nose knows. If something's calling, follow it.

Yoni steaming is a culturally-borrowed practice our modern world almost forgot. Honor the lineage. Don't rush it. This isn't a hack — it's slow, attentive time with the part of you that brings life and wisdom.
