A little patch, growing slowly

I propagate here in Humboldt the same way I tend everything — slow, organic, and a few trays at a time. Natives and perennials, edible starts, and cut flowers from my own field, all grown without anything I wouldn't want in my own beds.

This is a small, on-the-side thing, so I'll offer a little of each as it's ready rather than promising a full shelf. If you'd like first pick, I'm happy to send a note when the first batch comes in.
