The Succession Planting Seed Kit

Sixteen cases. One for every two-week window from February to September. Pull a case, plant what's inside, come back in two weeks.

Pre-curated for the coastal fog belt

The idea

Succession planting works, but it's mentally heavy. You have to remember what to sow, when, and what gets re-sown two weeks later. The Succession Kit takes the remembering off your shoulders. Sixteen pre-loaded compartments in a photo storage case. One per fortnight, from cold-hardy openers in February to garlic + cover crops in September.

Curated for Humboldt

Every variety chosen for our cool summers, mild winters, and marine layer. No hot-weather crops that need a hoop house just to ripen.

This Week pointer

Built-in date formula tells you exactly which case to pull right now. Open the spreadsheet, get this week's plan in one glance.

Re-sow notes

Each crop tells you when to sow it next. Lettuce in Case 2? Resow in Case 4. No more spreadsheet juggling.

Sixteen cases, eight months

A look at the full year. The pink-highlighted card is what you'd be planting right now.

What's in the box

  • Welcome cover with the kit philosophy and how to use the photo storage case
  • Case Index: 16 sowing windows mapped to dates and themes
  • Seed Inventory: every variety pre-curated, with source links and notes specific to coastal Humboldt
  • This Week pointer: live formula that updates daily and tells you which case to pull
  • Case Labels: print-and-stick labels for the photo storage organizer (cases sold separately)
  • Plus a credits page linking to my favorite seed sources for re-stocking

California seed sources

Every variety in the kit comes from a California seed company growing organic, heirloom, or both. Buying in-state means seed already adapted to our conditions, shorter shipping, and dollars staying close to home. Most local first.

  • Lavender Tyme Farm Humboldt County, CA

    Andrea's small Humboldt farm. Locally-grown lavender, herbs, and small-batch seed. As local as it gets, and tested in the same fog belt as your garden.

  • Peaceful Valley / Grow Organic Grass Valley, CA

    Organic seeds, cover crops, garlic and shallots, plus tools and amendments. The one-stop California shop for fall cover and bulb plantings.

  • Kitazawa Seed Company Oakland, CA

    Founded 1917. The country's oldest Asian vegetable seed company. Heirloom mizuna, tatsoi, pak choi, daikon, shiso. Perfect for our overwintering greens cases.

  • Renee's Garden Seeds Felton, CA

    Curated heirloom vegetable, flower, and herb varieties. Beautifully written packets. Region-tested in coastal Northern California, so the picks already know what fog feels like.

  • Sustainable Seed Company Covelo, CA

    Family-run, organic and heirloom only. Strong selection of beans, lettuces, and grains. Mendocino-based, so varieties are tested in conditions much like ours.

  • Larner Seeds Bolinas, CA

    California native plant seeds, sustainably wild-collected. Where to look for native cover crop alternatives, edible natives, and pollinator wildflowers.
