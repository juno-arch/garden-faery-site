A Little of My Magic

Here's what I can do for your garden, your bees, and your critters.

Garden Tending & Weeding

I come to your garden on a regular schedule and take care of everything: weeding, pruning, planting, mulching, watering, and seasonal upkeep. Morning shifts run 9am to 2pm (5 hours) and afternoon shifts run 2:30 to 4:30pm (2 hours). I treat every garden like it's my own, and I'll let you know if I spot anything that needs attention. Think of it as having your own personal garden faery on call.

$25/hr · Cash only · Book a visit

Creative Raised Beds

Not every raised bed has to be a cedar box. I build beds from all kinds of materials: cedar, woven willow, stacked firewood, salvaged bricks, whatever you have around or whatever fits your space. Every bed is sized to your yard and comes filled with quality soil from Mad River Gardens. I can plant them out with the herbs, veggies, or flowers of your choice. I recommend keeping beds 4 feet wide or narrower so you can comfortably reach the center without stepping in.

Custom pricing per project

Bee Sitting, Swarms & Hive Care

Whether you have a hive that needs looking after while you're away, a swarm that showed up uninvited, or you're curious about starting your own bees, I can help. I do gentle swarm relocation (no chemicals, no harm), regular hive health checks, and bee sitting for beekeepers who travel. Your bees are in good hands.

Swarm relocation often free · Other rates by situation

Pet & Plant Sitting

Going out of town? I'll keep your plants watered, your garden tended, and your pets fed and loved while you're away. We'll put together a care plan before you leave so everything runs smooth. I'm comfortable with dogs, cats, chickens, and goats. Have a different kind of critter? Send me care notes and we can talk. You come home to happy animals and a garden that looks like you never left.

$25/hr or flat rate · Let's chat
